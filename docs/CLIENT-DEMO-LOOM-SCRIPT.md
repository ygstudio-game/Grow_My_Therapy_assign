# Client Demo & Loom Walkthrough Script (5 Minutes)
**Target Persona:** Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD (Licensed Clinical Psychologist, Santa Monica, CA)  
**Tone:** Warm, professional, confident, consultative, non-technical  
**Evaluation Weight:** 30% of total assignment grade

---

## Quick Setup Before Recording
1. **Browser Windows:**
   - Window 1: Desktop view (1440px or full screen) at `http://localhost:3000` or `https://grow-my-therapy-clone-dun.vercel.app`
   - Window 2 (or DevTools toggle `Ctrl+Shift+M`): Mobile view (iPhone 14 / 375px width)
2. **Loom Camera:** Camera bubble in the bottom corner, clean background, natural eye contact.
3. **Key Goal:** Frame everything from Dr. Maya Reynolds' perspective—how each section builds trust, resonates with her ideal clients (high-achieving professionals and trauma survivors), and guides them gently toward scheduling a consultation.

---

## Minute-by-Minute Speaking Guide

### ⏱️ 0:00 – 0:45 | Welcome & Brand Atmosphere (Hero Section)
- **Visual:** Desktop view at the top of the homepage.
- **What to say:**
  > *"Hi Dr. Reynolds, it's such a pleasure to share this first draft of your new website with you!*  
  > *When designing this space, our primary goal was to create a digital home that immediately feels grounding, warm, and restorative—mirroring the exact feeling a client gets when they step into your Santa Monica practice.*  
  > *Up here in the hero section, we chose an earthy, natural color palette—warm sage greens, soft cream, and terracotta accents—to evoke calm and steady support. The headline, 'Anxiety & Trauma Therapy in Santa Monica,' speaks directly to your core specialties, paired with your professional portrait on the left and a prominent, welcoming 'Book a Free Consultation' button. A prospective client immediately knows who you are, where you practice, and how to take that first comfortable step."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:45 – 1:45 | Validating the Client's Experience (Trust-Building & Who I Help)
- **Visual:** Scroll smoothly down past the Hero to Trust-Building and the "Who I Help" cards.
- **What to say:**
  > *"As we scroll down, we immediately validate what brought them here. Many of your clients are high-achievers who look like they have everything together on the outside, but internally feel overwhelmed or stuck in overthinking. This section normalizes their experience right away.*  
  > *Next, we introduce 'Who I Help' with three beautifully distinct focus cards: High-Achieving Professionals, Trauma Survivors, and Entrepreneurs & Creatives. By breaking down your ideal client groups with clean visual cues, visitors don't have to guess whether you're the right therapist for them—they immediately see themselves and feel understood."*

---

### ⏱️ 1:45 – 2:30 | Depth, Insight & Clinical Specialties
- **Visual:** Scroll past the full-bleed Quote Band to the Expertise Grid, About section, and Services cards.
- **What to say:**
  > *"We then feature a peaceful quote band highlighting your philosophy: 'Therapy isn't just about symptom relief—it's about insight, resilience, and coming home to yourself.'*  
  > *Directly below, your 'Areas of Expertise' are presented in an elegant two-column layout with subtle dividers, showcasing your modalities like CBT, EMDR, and body-oriented therapy.*  
  > *In your 'About' section, we introduce your background and collaborative approach, pairing your bio with an authentic photo to establish deep personal rapport. Following that, we showcase your three core service pathways—Anxiety & Panic Treatment, Trauma Therapy & EMDR, and Burnout Support—each with dedicated 'Learn more' links to guide them deeper into your offerings."*

---

### ⏱️ 2:30 – 3:30 | The New "Our Office" Experience & Clarifying FAQs
- **Visual:** Scroll to the "Our Office" section, pause on the two office photos, then scroll to the FAQ accordion.
- **What to say:**
  > *"One special feature we custom-crafted specifically for your practice is the 'Our Office' section. We know how much physical environment matters for therapy, so we dedicated a full section to spotlighting your quiet, sunlit Santa Monica office.*  
  > *We've featured real photos of your peaceful seating area and reading corner alongside your physical address and session availability—reassuring clients that whether they see you in-person in Santa Monica or via secure telehealth across California, they are in safe hands.*  
  > *Right below that, we have an interactive Frequently Asked Questions accordion. Prospective clients can easily click open questions about session types, trauma pacing, and modalities—addressing common hesitations upfront before they even reach out."*

---

### ⏱️ 3:30 – 4:20 | Mobile Experience & Seamless Usability
- **Visual:** Switch to Mobile View (375px) / Responsive Device Mode in DevTools.
- **What to say:**
  > *"More than 60% of people searching for a therapist do so from their phones during quiet moments, so mobile experience was top-of-mind.*  
  > *Notice how cleanly everything reflows: our header features a clean, accessible mobile menu; the hero headline and call-to-action remain front and center without horizontal scrolling; the cards and office photos stack naturally in a relaxed vertical rhythm; and buttons are generously sized for effortless touch interaction on any smartphone."*

---

### ⏱️ 4:20 – 5:00 | Call-to-Action, Closing & Next Steps
- **Visual:** Scroll to the final CTA Band ('Ready to feel more like yourself again?') and the Footer.
- **What to say:**
  > *"Finally, we conclude with a bold, inviting closing invitation to 'Book a Free Consultation' and a clean footer featuring your practice navigation, verified Santa Monica location, California telehealth availability, and an accessible consultation request button.*  
  > *Overall, this site was designed to be not just a website, but an extension of your healing practice—warm, organized, trustworthy, and welcoming.*  
  > *Thank you so much, Dr. Reynolds! I'd love to hear your thoughts, and we can make any fine-tuning adjustments together. Looking forward to our next steps!"*
